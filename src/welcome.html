Talk to your AI assistants

    OpenPosse lets you talk to the AI assistants in the tools you already use, by voice. Everything it hears and says stays on this device.

    Let it hear you

    Chrome will ask once, in this tab. The microphone is given to OpenPosse itself, not to any website.

    Download the speech model

    Chrome turns what you say into words on this device, so it needs its speech model. It downloads once.

    Pin OpenPosse

    Pin it so it's one click away: open the puzzle piece next to the address bar and press the pin beside OpenPosse. This ticks itself when you do.

    Your shortcut

    Many people pick ⌥V and tick Global, so it works even when Chrome is in the background.

    Keep its tabs together

    OpenPosse can keep the tabs it talks to in one tab group, called OpenPosse, so they're easy to find. It never opens or closes a tab, and leaves tabs in your own groups where they are. You can change this in Settings.
