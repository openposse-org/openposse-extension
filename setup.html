Set up OpenPosse

Three things, once. Everything after this happens on your machine — speech recognition included.

1

Let it hear you

The microphone is granted to OpenPosse itself, not to any website, so this is asked once and never again. It has to be asked on a page like this one — a background worker has nowhere to show the prompt.

not granted
2

Download the speech model

A few tens of megabytes, once. After this, nothing you say is sent anywhere — recognition runs entirely on this machine, and OpenPosse requires it to.

checking…
3

Choose how you summon it

Chrome only lets an extension suggest Ctrl+Shift and a digit for a shortcut that works outside the browser, so OpenPosse ships with one of those. Anything else — ⌥V, say — you pick here, and you can tick Global so it reaches you in any app rather than only in Chrome.

checking…
4

Show the panel on any page — optional

The panel appears over whatever page you're on. Chrome normally lends an extension access to the current tab when you press its shortcut — but not when that shortcut is Global, which is exactly what makes ⌥V work from other apps. So the two can't both be had without asking you directly.

Chrome will word this as “Read and change all your data on all websites”, because that is the only wording it has. What OpenPosse does with it is draw the panel. It still sends nothing anywhere — speech, assistants and answers all stay on this machine — and it does not read pages it hasn't been asked to drive.

Skip it and everything still works: the panel appears on your assistants' own pages, and the toolbar icon shows what's happening everywhere else. You can turn it off again here at any time.

5

Bring across assistants you already recorded — optional

If you used the OpenPosse Mac app, pick its folder and the assistants come with their recipes intact: ~/Library/Application Support/OpenPosse
